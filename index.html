I’m an M.S. student in Electrical Engineering at KAIST and a visiting researcher at Seoul National University, working with Prof. Jaemin Yoo on a foundation model for generative recommendation. I’m also a research fellow at Equitech Futures, studying how to evaluate and reduce sycophancy in large language models.

My research interests include recommender systems, graph neural networks, and machine learning. Previously, I worked on graph collaborative filtering, prompt optimization, and time-series imputation. I completed my B.S. in Computer Science with a minor in AI at KAIST in August 2025.
